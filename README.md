# StateSSC

Camera-based temporal semantic scene completion (SSC) uses historical observations to recover scene content missing from the current view. Although pose alignment establishes spatial correspondences across frames, historical candidate information can still exhibit different geometric relationships with the currently observed surface. Existing temporal methods often compress these candidates into a single representation, thereby obscuring their distinct roles in current-scene inference.To address this limitation, we propose a current-centric SSC framework for improving current-scene inference through more effective use of historical information. Specifically, it compares pose-aligned historical depth candidates with the current depth distribution and assigns each candidate to one of three geometric states based on its position relative to the current surface: front, surface or behind. To preserve these states during voxelization, we design a State-Aware Voxel Aggregation (SAVA) module that performs intra-frame voxelization and inter-frame aggregation. We further introduce a State-Guided History Refinement (SGHR) module that refines historical voxel representations according to their states and fuses them with current-frame voxel representations for the final voxel representation. Experiments on SemanticKITTI and SSCBench-KITTI-360 demonstrate that our method achieves competitive performance against existing SSC methods in both geometric and semantic completion.
